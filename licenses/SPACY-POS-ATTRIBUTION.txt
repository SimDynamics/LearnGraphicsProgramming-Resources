MurmurHash algorithms by Austin Appleby are in the public domain.
The selected Thinc CPU uint64 specialization is ported faithfully to Kotlin.

Modifications: native Kotlin tokenizer/lexical extraction and a local ONNX opset 14
conversion of the original float32 tok2vec and tagger parameters. The encoder's
four padding rows are retained through all residual blocks. No retraining or
quantization is performed. The optional converted graphs and resources are
installed separately with their pinned SHA-256 and are not bundled into the APK.
Python, spaCy, Thinc, typer-slim and click are used only during conversion;
their native Python extensions are not redistributed in the Android application.

source exactly after spaCy string hashing. They remain available after optional
Play delivery is removed and for the original resources retained by the application.
