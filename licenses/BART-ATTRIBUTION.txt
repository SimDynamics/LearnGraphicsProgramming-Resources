Modifications: local conversion of the original encoder and decoder to ONNX
opset 17, full float32, without quantization or retraining. Kotlin implements
the original greedy generation and dialect-specific token mapping. The source
model cards declare Apache-2.0. Converted resources are optional separate
installations verified against the fixed sizes and SHA-256 in EnglishAuxResources.

The Python conversion tools and their development dependencies are not shipped
in the Android application. No eSpeak fallback is distributed or used.
