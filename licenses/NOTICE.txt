Modifications: the original BART weights were locally converted to separate
encoder/decoder ONNX opset 17 graphs. The original spaCy tok2vec and tagger
weights were locally converted to an ONNX opset 14 graph with a JSON frontend.
These are full FP32 conversions, without quantization or retraining. This
pack preserves the eight already verified artifact bytes and their pinned
SHA-256 identifiers; packaging does not regenerate or alter the models.

This pack contains no Kokoro shared acoustic model, voice weights, English
pronunciation dictionaries, Python runtime, model training data, or complete
spaCy pipeline. OntoNotes 5 was licensed by Explosion for training; its corpus
is not redistributed. The upstream model is released under MIT. The
lemmatizer and its WordNet resources are excluded from this POS conversion.
